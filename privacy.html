Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Footing is published by the developer named as the seller on its App Store page. Contact: support@footingdbt.com

1. The short version

  • Footing has no account. What you write stays on your phone.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your diary entries, notes, ratings, skills or safety plan.
  • You choose what to share. The weekly summary and backup files are created on your phone and only leave it when you use the share sheet.
  • Footing is a self-help companion. It is not medical advice or a crisis service.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Your diary entries (ratings, skills used, notes), the diary card items you choose, your safety plan, and your reminder setting.
  • Before an app update changes how data is stored, Footing keeps one safety copy of your diary in the app's private storage so nothing is lost if an update goes wrong. It is replaced by the next safety copy and erased by Delete all my data.
  • This data is in the app's storage on your phone. iPhone and iCloud device backups may include it, depending on your Apple settings.

3. What we collect

Nothing about your use of the app. We do not run analytics or advertising, and we do not use tracking.

4. Purchases

Footing is a paid app: you pay once, through Apple, when you download it. We do not receive your payment details or any information about your purchase beyond what Apple shares with developers (such as sales totals). Footing has no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no purchase software inside the app.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Footing schedules a local notification on your phone. No notification data is sent to us.

6. Sharing and exports

  • The weekly summary (PDF) and backup (JSON) are generated on your phone. They contain your diary information. Once you share a file, the receiving app or person controls it.
  • You decide whether notes are included in the weekly summary.

7. Your choices

  • Delete everything from More > Delete all my data. This permanently erases your data on this device.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your phone. A device backup you made earlier may still contain a copy.
  • Turn off reminders in More or in your phone settings.

8. Children

Footing is not directed to children under 13.

9. Safety and emergencies

Footing cannot monitor you or contact anyone for you. If you might hurt yourself or someone else, call your local emergency number. The in-app Support screen lists verified emergency numbers and crisis lines for several countries (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand) and links to a free international directory for everywhere else.

10. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change affects what information leaves your phone, we will also say so in the app's update notes before it takes effect.

11. Contact
